## Proposed research direction

**AI for Automotive Fault Diagnosis and Predictive Maintenance: A Scoping Review of Applicability to Nigeria**

The proposed question is: What AI approaches have been studied for automotive fault diagnosis and predictive maintenance, how have they been evaluated, and what further evidence is needed to assess applicability to Nigerian vehicle-maintenance settings?

The study would map published evidence and identify gaps. International studies would inform questions for local investigation; their results would not establish performance in Nigeria.

## How AI is used

AI assistants help draft structure, suggest search terms, and organize source information. Sources are attributed to their authors. Each note must state what was actually checked; an abstract summary is not a full-paper appraisal. This initial workspace was prepared with Codex assistance and should be reviewed and developed by the student.
