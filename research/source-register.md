## S01 — background

The abstract describes data science, machine learning, and optimization across the automotive value chain. It supports background terminology and a broad topic map. This early source does not establish current Nigerian adoption, present-day deployment costs, or local effectiveness.

## S02 — candidate primary study

The abstract describes combining diagnostic trouble-code sequences with environmental context in BiCarFormer, a Transformer-based approach to vehicle error-pattern classification. It reports a real-world dataset and improved classification relative to comparator approaches. The abstract alone does not establish the validation split, data accessibility, exact effect sizes, or applicability to Nigeria. Full-text assessment is required before inclusion or technical appraisal.
